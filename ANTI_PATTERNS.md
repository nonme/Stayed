## Anti-AI Style Rules

### Banned Phrases
Verbatim or close variants:

"it's worth noting", "it's important to note", "it's crucial to", "notably", "importantly",
"under the hood", "real-world", "table stakes", "game-changer", "seamless", "seamlessly",
"out of the box", "at the end of the day", "think of it like", "think of X as Y",
"delve into", "deep dive", "navigate", "at the heart of", "landscape", "ecosystem",
"leverage" (as verb), "robust", "holistic", "nuanced", "multifaceted",
"moreover", "furthermore", "crucially", "foster", "streamlined"

Fantasy-specific:
"a shiver ran down", "his/her heart clenched/lurched/hammered",
"something shifted inside", "the air felt heavy", "he/she hadn't realized until now",
"ancient and terrible", "chosen one", "darkness pressed in",
"nothing would ever be the same", "fate had other plans"

---

### Banned Structural Patterns

General:
- Perfect antithesis: "It's not X, it's Y" / "This isn't just X — it's Y" / "Not about A, but about B"
- Rhetorical fragments as transitions: "The result?", "The goal?", "Why does this matter?"
- Tricolon padding: grouping ideas in threes for rhythm rather than meaning
- Hedging chains: stacking "often", "typically", "generally", "can be", "may" without a specific claim
- Throat-clearing openers: "Let's dive in", "Let's explore", "Let's break this down", "In this section"
- Generic specificity: examples that sound concrete but contain no actual detail ("in a production environment", "at scale")

Narrative / Fiction:
- Emotion signaled by physiological cliché instead of action or concrete detail
- Characters explaining their own feelings in dialogue ("I'm angry because you betrayed me")
- Ping-pong dialogue: each line directly answers the previous one, no interruption, no misdirection
- Uniform pacing: every scene and beat given equal descriptive weight
- On-cue character transformation: change arrives exactly when the plot requires it, without resistance
- Generic fantasy shorthand: ancient evil awakening, destiny realized, power flowing through veins
- Threshold moments over-narrated: the character pauses, reflects, and understands — every time

English prose patterns:
- The italicized single word as paragraph: *He ran. Fast.* Too fast.
- Em-dash as default tension beat: "She turned — and stopped."
- Present-tense interiority interrupting past-tense action to explain what the character feels
- Stacked short sentences to simulate urgency without actual narrative compression
